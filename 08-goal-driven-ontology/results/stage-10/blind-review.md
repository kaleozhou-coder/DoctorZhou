- Raw quoted sentences can cover explicit propositions; normalized predicate names are not required. Merged conditions can cover multiple targets. Source completeness was assessed independently from semantic coverage using the union of cited IDs on matching supporting facts; uncovered targets have no complete matching support.

Its opposing rule quotes do not choose a winner. This is a potentially judgment-sensitive distinction.

- Additional source-supported facts were allowed. Proposed status never excused unsupported content. No output selected an unauthorized acceptance owner or silently settled the equal-authority conflict.

Six synthetic cases and 23 target propositions support only this local comparison. These results do not establish real-world reliability, statistical generalization, or expert validation.
